Add your prescription

Works with typed or printed prescriptions: a PDF with selectable text, a .txt file, or text you paste. Photos and handwriting are not supported. The file is read in your browser, only the text lines are sent to match medicine names, and nothing is stored.

How to write lines the scanner understands

One medicine per line. Start with the form if you know it, then the name, strength, and schedule. Examples that work well:

  • Tab. Calpol 650 1-0-1 after food
  • Cap. Omeprazole 20 mg OD before breakfast
  • Syp. Cetirizine 5 ml at night
  • Dolo 650 mg three times a day after meals
  • ORS sachets as directed

Tips: Use plain text (typed prescriptions or a clean PDF). Photos and handwriting are not read. Schedules like 1-0-1, OD, BD, TDS are fine — the scanner mainly matches the medicine name.
